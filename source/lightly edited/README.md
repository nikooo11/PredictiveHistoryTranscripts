# Predictive History — Conservative Editorial Transcript Edition

This is a separate editorial reading edition of the 191-file transcript archive. Every transcript begins with its source YouTube URL and then contains only the edited transcript text—no internal labels, notes, summaries, or commentary.

## Editing standard

This edition applies **only auditable, deterministic corrections** to the prior readable transcript edition. The rules remove speech fillers, repair known unambiguous ASR fragments, normalize paragraph whitespace and punctuation spacing, standardize `U.S.-Canada`, and correct clearly dangling grammar where the surrounding sentence makes the intended construction explicit. The scripts do not generate new claims, add context, remove substantive passages, alter dates/names/numbers, paraphrase arguments, or change the sequence of ideas.
