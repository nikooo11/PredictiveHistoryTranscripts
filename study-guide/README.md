## 1. How it ties together: religion + politics → predicting the future → investing

Below is his own framework in his own words. The chain he keeps returning to is this: **religion and myth create the will and coordination of peoples, then political power and empire organise that will, then money and finance turn it into wealth and leverage. Model all three and you can forecast.** He tests the model by making public predictions.

*Quotes are his words as they appear in the repo's YouTube auto-captions (lightly trimmed with …). Captions can misspell names and mishear words.*

## Method, sources and gaps

- **Lecturer:** the repo's README and CLAUDE.md identify the channel as **Professor Jiang Xueqin's *Predictive History*** (YouTube). Lectures were given to students (some in Beijing), so students, guests and callers also speak.
- **Corpus:** 191 catalogued videos, of which **175 are distinct** (16 are re-uploads/duplicates). That is 35,760 caption paragraphs and about 1.9 million words. Every video URL and timestamp link in this guide comes from the repo (`catalog.json` and transcript files). **No links were invented; none were 'not found'.**
- **How the transcripts were read:** every one of the 175 distinct transcripts was loaded and processed in full with scripts. Keyword, cue and regex passes covered books, dates, predictions and money. The scripts surfaced passages, and those passages were read (thousands of context lines), together with every video's description, which he writes himself. In honesty: at ~1.9M words, **not every line of every transcript was read word-for-word by eye.** Conclusions rest on the scripted extraction plus reading of the extracted passages. The 12 Dante seminar recordings (2.5-4 hours each, heavy on reading aloud) were sampled the same way.
- **Caption caveats:** auto-captions mangle names (Thucydides becomes 'fusidities', the Aeneid becomes 'iniad', Dugin becomes 'Dugan', Jiang becomes 'Jang/Tang'). They often render 'World War III' as 'World War II'. 74 pre-July-2025 videos have unpunctuated captions. Quotes are reproduced as captioned.
- **Books:** a book counts for a video if it is named in the transcript or cited in his description's reference list (marked *d*). Obvious false positives were removed by hand (e.g. 'Republican' ≠ Plato's Republic; a chat user named Karina ≠ Anna Karenina). Counts are approximate.
- **Record checks:** accepted dates are standard historical dates. Events after 2025 (the 2026 US-Iran war, Khamenei's death, Mojtaba Khamenei's succession, the blockade and ceasefire status, no ground invasion as of 20 Oct 2026) were checked against news reports (NPR, NBC, Reuters, Bloomberg/Rigzone, CBS) via web search on 20 Oct 2026. Other recent claims are marked 'not verified here'.
- **Controversial material:** he discusses secret societies, Sabbatean-Frankist theories, 'Pax Judaica', and doubts about the Moon landing, JFK and 9/11. He sometimes labels this 'conspiracy theory... just for fun'. This guide reports it as *his* framing and notes where the record differs. It does not endorse it.
- **Existing `guide/` folder** in the repo (a separate work-in-progress) was not modified.
