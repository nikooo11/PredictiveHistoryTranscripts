## The story he tells

Since his first Geo-Strategy class in April 2024, Jiang has told one story about this part of the world: America will be lured into a ground war in Iran that it cannot win, and the defeat will end its empire. That April he said a re-elected Trump would probably start the war "maybe two years from now" [GS 1 · 43:19](https://youtu.be/xEEpOxqdU5E?t=2599). In his telling, three forces push America toward war: the Israel lobby (Jewish and Christian Zionist), America's addiction to the easy money of empire, and Saudi Arabia. All three work through Trump and his son-in-law Jared Kushner (see Chapter 11) [GS 8 · 00:00](https://youtu.be/7y_hbz6loEo?t=0) [GS 8 · 02:08](https://youtu.be/7y_hbz6loEo?t=128). Iran's Revolutionary Guards, meanwhile, want the Americans to come [GS 8 · 10:43](https://youtu.be/7y_hbz6loEo?t=643). The result is his "Iran trap": mountains turn invaders into hostages, sunk-cost thinking keeps them there, and Putin's nuclear umbrella takes away America's way out [GS 8 · 1:00:07](https://youtu.be/7y_hbz6loEo?t=3607). He argues that Israel and Saudi Arabia gain most if America and Iran wreck each other [GS 8 · 57:26](https://youtu.be/7y_hbz6loEo?t=3446).

The other leaders fit around that core. Putin is the patient strategist who uses the chaos to break the dollar [GT 27 · 32:57](https://youtu.be/x83HcLWvHI8?t=1977). Xi's China is a trading power that wants stability and will lean toward America in the end [GT 27 · 1:09:26](https://youtu.be/x83HcLWvHI8?t=4166). From mid-2025 he adds religion. Trump, Khamenei and Netanyahu each feel a "messianic calling" [GSU 3 · 03:39](https://youtu.be/bc9adtiIN_k?t=219), and the end-times scripts of Shia Islam, Judaism, Christian Zionism and Russian Orthodoxy "converge" on a Middle Eastern world war [GSU 7 · 30:34](https://youtu.be/YQ-xg1nIbMs?t=1834). (The captions usually turn "World War III" into "World War II".)

Dates matter here. The US bombed Iran's nuclear sites in June 2025, and he called that the start of World War III [GSU 2 · 00:01](https://youtu.be/N4cs-8mrP_s?t=1). A full US–Israeli war began at the end of February 2026 with a strike that, by his account, killed Khamenei [GT 9 · 00:58](https://youtu.be/jIS2eB-rGv0?t=58). An April ceasefire followed; he says Pakistan mediated it under Chinese pressure [Meet-Up 1 · 30:26](https://youtu.be/zbjLl85FLUc?t=1826). By July it had collapsed [Emergency 1 · 1:56:03](https://youtu.be/A9Sr-4c-3Tg?t=6963). The American ground invasion he has predicted since 2024 had still not come by August 2026. He then said it would follow the November midterms [Meet-Up 2 · 1:24:19](https://youtu.be/N2GFDDZtGQY?t=5059). Events after mid-2025 are given here as he describes them.

## The main figures

### Ruhollah Khomeini

*Who:* Shia cleric (1902–1989) who led Iran's 1979 Islamic Revolution and was its first Supreme Leader until his death.

**What he teaches**
- Khomeini created the Revolutionary Guards because he could not trust the army, which had sworn allegiance to the Shah. The army's duty was to protect the nation; the Guards' duty was to protect the revolution, which in practice made them his private army [GS 7 · 12:57](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=777) [GS 7 · 14:03](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=843).
- He backed the students who seized the US embassy in 1979. Jiang dwells on how they pieced shredded files back together and concluded that the embassy, not the Shah, had really run Iran [GS 7 · 22:46](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1366) [GS 7 · 24:55](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1495).
- Against Saddam's tanks he set faith. Boys of 16 to 18 in the Basij, each given a rifle and a key to heaven, ran across Iraqi minefields until the Iraqi army ran out of ammunition and energy [GS 7 · 27:13](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1633) [GS 7 · 28:21](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1701).

**In his big picture:** Khomeini is where the martyrdom-minded military culture comes from, the culture Jiang thinks makes Iran unconquerable and its Guards eager for war.

**In his words:** "we don't have weapons but we have God on our side" [GS 7 · 27:13](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1633) (Jiang voicing Khomeini's message to the volunteers)

### Ali Khamenei

*Who:* Shia cleric (born 1939) who succeeded Khomeini as Supreme Leader in 1989, with final say over Iran's military, judiciary and foreign policy. The captions usually call him just "the Ayatollah".

**What he teaches**
- The Supreme Leader holds real power: he represents God and the Quran, with a veto over everything, while "the president is maybe like the CEO of the country" [GS 7 · 09:19](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=559).
- Khamenei built his rule on the Guards. From 1989 he met their roughly 2,000 officers and memorized their names and even their children's names, so that "they're basically his private Army" [GS 7 · 38:04](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=2284).
- In June 2025 Jiang gave him a "messianic calling". At 86 and hunted by Mossad and the US military, Khamenei feels "ecstatic" at the chance, awaited all his life, to fight the Great Satan. Zoroastrian good-versus-evil dualism and the Shia ideal of martyrdom shape his aim: to draw America into Iran and destroy it there [GSU 3 · 12:38](https://youtu.be/bc9adtiIN_k?t=758) [GSU 3 · 14:30](https://youtu.be/bc9adtiIN_k?t=870) [GSU 3 · 15:23](https://youtu.be/bc9adtiIN_k?t=923).
- Before the 2026 war he named Khamenei's assassination as a key unknown, one that would set off a succession crisis and probably help America and Israel [GSU 1 · 14:25](https://youtu.be/8XdL-7tAqnU?t=865) [GSU 7 · 00:25](https://youtu.be/YQ-xg1nIbMs?t=25).
- Afterwards he read the killing as martyrdom, not decapitation. In his account (March 3, 2026), the strike killed Khamenei with his daughter, son-in-law and grandchildren, and Iranian media said he had refused to flee to Moscow. For Shia believers, he argues, that made the war a jihad of vengeance [GT 9 · 02:06](https://youtu.be/jIS2eB-rGv0?t=126) [GT 9 · 03:48](https://youtu.be/jIS2eB-rGv0?t=228).
- So his death did not break Iran. A long-sanctioned elite with nothing to lose closed ranks, and the decentralized "mosaic defense" works like a dead man's switch [GT 11 · 50:38](https://youtu.be/fz-Dan7NRss?t=3038) [GT 20 · 04:33](https://youtu.be/ue8y5e3HnHE?t=273). In July 2026 he describes a funeral where millions cried out for vengeance [Live 2 · 1:01:05](https://youtu.be/E7QKiRnw0M8?t=3665).

**In his big picture:** Khamenei is the Iranian pole of the "eschatological convergence", a leader who wants the final battle as much as America's hawks do. That is why Jiang expects the Iran trap to close.

**In his words:** "he wants to lure the great Satan into Iran" [GSU 3 · 15:23](https://youtu.be/bc9adtiIN_k?t=923)

**Reality check:** For decades most analysts described Khamenei as avoiding direct war with the United States. His own 2018 formula was "no war, no negotiation." The eager warrior is Jiang's psychological reading.

### Ebrahim Raisi

*Who:* Iranian cleric and former judiciary chief (1960–2024), president from 2021 until he died in a helicopter crash on May 19, 2024.

**What he teaches**
- GS 7, taught five days after the crash, is a game-theory exercise. The likeliest cause is an accident: fog and an old American-built helicopter that sanctions left Iran unable to maintain. Of the alternatives, a foreign enemy lacked opportunity and motive; an internal enemy had both [GS 7 · 03:38](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=218) [GS 7 · 04:43](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=283) [GS 7 · 07:00](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=420).
- The internal suspect is the IRGC. Raisi was expected to succeed Khamenei, and a new Supreme Leader would build his own power base by cutting the Guards down. With Raisi gone, Mojtaba becomes likelier [GS 7 · 08:06](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=486) [GS 7 · 34:55](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=2095).
- He casts Raisi as the voice of restraint, which he thinks explains Iran's muted response to Soleimani's killing. After that and the April 2024 Damascus strike, Jiang imagines Raisi telling the Guards that Iran could not beat America and Israel and had to be patient [GS 7 · 33:01](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=1981) [GS 7 · 33:56](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=2036).
- He attached a test. If the Guards did it, vice president Mohammad Mokhber, whom he places in the Guards' camp, should win the June election, rhetoric should harden, and the proxies should escalate. **In his big picture:** Raisi's death is his first worked example of reasoning from motive and opportunity when facts are scarce. It also sets up the IRGC as the actor that wants war.

**In his words:** "it was probably an accident but if it was not an accident then who would most benefit from killing him" [GS 7 · 47:53](https://youtu.be/LsRGvKxfLts?t=2873)

**Reality check:** Iran's official investigation blamed the weather and found no sign of sabotage. His test also failed: Mokhber did not run, and the reformist Masoud Pezeshkian won the July 2024 election. The lectures never go back to the prediction.
