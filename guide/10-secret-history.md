# Chapter 10: The Secret History: Hidden Networks in His Telling

*Draws mainly on the Secret History course (#1, #4–6, #10, #23–27 and #END, August–December 2025), plus Geo-Strategy Update #7, Game Theory #12, #16 and #26, and the August 2026 Global Meet-Ups #2–3.*

## The story he tells

The Secret History course, taught to his Beijing high-school students from August to December 2025, starts from a blunt premise: the history taught in school is "a system implanted into your brains by powerful people" [SH 1 · 06:07](https://youtu.be/lt8XLz78ZvY?t=367). The real actors, he argues, are secret societies. His mechanism comes from game theory. In a winner-takes-all game, whoever coordinates secretly wins, and the strongest glue is shared transgression, crimes that make members unable to betray each other. Visible rulers are "puppets they are just the face" [SH 4 · 37:37](https://youtu.be/BtlWoqWLm9Q?t=2257). He says the societies began as mystery schools guarding a Gnostic secret, that the god of the Bible is a false god and a divine spark lives in each person, and were later corrupted into instruments of power [SH 5 · 22:15](https://youtu.be/EgGfQdv_rPc?t=1335), [SH 5 · 40:25](https://youtu.be/EgGfQdv_rPc?t=2425).

The later lectures build a lineage. Paul's Church lets Rome's old dynasties, the "black nobility", store their power. Merchant republics from Venice to the Dutch Republic to the Bank of England create "transnational capital", which he treats as the same thing as secret societies [SH 25 · 10:31](https://youtu.be/B5FtHagng8c?t=631). The protagonists of modernity, in his telling, are the Sabbatean Frankists, a heretical messianic sect whose creed of "justification by sin" made them ideal agents for the British Empire [SH 27 · 05:39](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=339). The finale, "Pax Judaica", merges everything. Freemasons, Illuminati, Frankists and Christian Zionists follow end-time scripts that converge on Jerusalem, where a coming war would leave Israel as the hub of an AI surveillance empire [SH END · 07:14](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=434), [SH END · 36:08](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=2168).

Three features shape how he tells it. He hedges constantly: "Some will call this conspiracy theory" [SH END · 02:15](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=135). He insists that Jews are not the hidden masters but "construct and tool of empire. In other words, they're really the middle managers" [SH 27 · 00:00](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=0). And his framing shifts over time. By May 2026 he calls the popular Templar-to-Illuminati lineage "anti-historical" [GT 26 · 05:59](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=359), and in August 2026 he describes secret societies as "a natural byproduct of transnational capitalism" rather than one continuous plot [Meet-Up 3 · 49:57](https://youtu.be/5rDMq-5_xkM?t=2997). Much of the material still restates long-standing conspiracy theories, several of them antisemitic in origin. The Reality checks below say where.

## The main figures

### Sabbatai Zevi

*Who:* An Ottoman Jewish mystic from Smyrna (İzmir), 1626–1676, proclaimed Messiah in 1665. His movement swept the Jewish world until he converted to Islam before the sultan in 1666.

**What he teaches**
- He sets Zevi in a crisis of faith after centuries of expulsions, when Jews asked why the loyal were punished and converts were not, and calls him "the equivalent of Jesus in the Jewish religion" [SH 26 · 19:07](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1147).
- In his reading Zevi was a revolutionary against the rabbis, whom he portrays as a corrupt elite in league with European nobles. Zevi wanted a faith without law or hierarchy [SH 26 · 20:53](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1253), [SH 26 · 22:09](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1329).
- He explains the conversion as a Christ-like sacrifice. Facing "the turban or your head", Zevi became Muslim to redeem every Jew who had ever converted and free them from the law [SH 26 · 22:57](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1377), [SH 26 · 23:40](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1420).
- He credits Zevi with the doctrine of "justification by sin": breaking the law proves true faith [SH 26 · 24:22](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1462).
- The families who followed Zevi into Islam, the Dönmeh ("Doma" in the captions), "will eventually come to control Turkey". He calls Atatürk one of them and says they founded the Republic and still run it [SH 26 · 25:40](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1540), [SH 27 · 10:49](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=649).

**In his big picture:** Zevi is the seed of the modern secret history, the moment a messianic, law-breaking current enters Jewish, Muslim and later Christian society in disguise.

**In his words:** "So in many ways he's a proto liberal and a protocommunist." [SH 26 · 22:09](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1329)

**Reality check:** The dates and the conversion are well documented. Reading Zevi as a proto-liberal goes beyond Gershom Scholem's own contested argument that Sabbateanism weakened rabbinic authority and eased the way to the Jewish Enlightenment. A few Young Turk figures, such as finance minister Mehmed Cavid, came from Dönmeh families, but the claims that Atatürk was Dönmeh or that the Dönmeh run Turkey are conspiracy theories common in Turkish Islamist and antisemitic writing, with no supporting evidence.

### Jacob Frank and the Frankists

*Who:* Jacob Frank (1726–1791), born in Podolia in the Polish Commonwealth, led a Sabbatean sect that claimed to continue Zevi's mission. After clashes with the rabbis, he and several hundred followers were baptized Catholic in 1759. The Church held him at Częstochowa from 1760 to 1773, and he died at Offenbach, near Frankfurt.

**What he teaches**
- Frank, in his account Zevi's reincarnation for his followers, turned the creed into "a faith based on success". If the material world is a corpse, why suffer in it, when you can "cheat, hack the system, and become kings ourselves" [SH 26 · 26:01](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1561), [SH 26 · 27:32](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1652), [SH 26 · 28:23](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1703).
- He teaches Frank's *Words of the Lord* as a manual like Machiavelli's *The Prince*, "a guide on how to conquer the world": people are "fundamentally stupid and superstitious" and can be tricked through their own rules, while the "company" of brothers and sisters must love one another [SH 26 · 29:22](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1762), [SH 26 · 32:48](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1968), [SH 26 · 42:32](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=2552).
- He stresses that Frankism is not Judaism but a rejection of it, and that Frank "hates Jews" [SH 26 · 27:17](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=1637), [SH 26 · 1:15:22](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4522).
- In his account Frankist rites (a naked group conversion ceremony, wife-swapping and, he claims, incest with their children) sealed a covenant and produced guilt that fuelled ambition; he even calls wife-swapping "women empowerment" [SH 26 · 1:10:03](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4203), [SH 26 · 1:11:12](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4272), [SH 26 · 1:11:38](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4298), [SH 26 · 1:14:58](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4498).
- After their baptism, he says, Frankists rose into the nobility, law and banking ("all the top lawyers in Warsaw are Frank"), and in their aim of ruling the world "They succeeded." [SH 26 · 48:36](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=2916), [SH 26 · 1:14:16](https://youtu.be/kULUM_eB8KI?t=4456).
- In SH 27 they become the British Empire's natural allies, a crypto-Jewish diaspora with capital, intelligence networks and revolutionary cells: "They believed they had to destroy the world in order to save it." He reads Marx's vanguard and Freud's retreat from the seduction theory as Frankist influence (see Chapter 9), while conceding "it may just be random coincidence" [SH 27 · 06:17](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=377), [SH 27 · 1:04:11](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=3851), [SH 27 · 1:19:01](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=4741), [SH 27 · 1:26:58](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=5218).
- In SH END Frank's wish for eternal bodily pleasure explains transhumanism. In June 2026 he adds that Frankists "like to promote anti-semitism in order to hide" behind ordinary Jews [SH END · 1:15:16](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=4516), [Substack Live 1 · 37:19](https://youtu.be/f03GAKlvZRs?t=2239).

**In his big picture:** The Frankists are the engine of his modern secret history, a sect whose theology of sin, secrecy and material conquest becomes the creed of transnational capital. He calls Frank "an evil guy" whose mind he understands from reading him [SH END · 1:36:22](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=5782).

**In his words:** "everything goes back to Jacob Frank" [SH END · 1:15:16](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=4516)

**Reality check:** The sect was real, and before their baptism Frank's followers even backed a blood-libel accusation against rabbinic Jews at the 1759 Lwów disputation. But historians find that it dissolved into Polish and Bohemian society within a few generations, and nothing links a Frankist network to the British Empire, Marx or Freud (Jeffrey Masson's critique of Freud, which he cites, makes no such claim). The modern Sabbatean-Frankist conspiracy theory circulates mainly in online conspiracy media, and researchers of antisemitism describe it as a relabelling of older claims of secret Jewish power; by his own account he began researching it after hearing about it from David Icke and Candace Owens [Members 2 · 2:22:23](https://youtu.be/qRCSZoGC2Dg?t=8543).

### The Rothschilds

*Who:* A banking dynasty founded by Mayer Amschel Rothschild (1744–1812) in Frankfurt, whose sons opened houses in London, Paris, Vienna and Naples. In the 19th century they were Europe's leading lenders to governments.

**What he teaches**

- He reads the 1917 Balfour letter to Lord Rothschild as Britain creating Israel while "blaming the Jews for it" [SH END · 49:29](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=2969).

**In his words:** "the Rothschild, for whatever reason, think they control the world" [SH 27 · 41:16](https://youtu.be/ZPrecJHUOUs?t=2476)

**Reality check:** Historians trace the Waterloo coup to an antisemitic French pamphlet of 1846, and studies of the family's records (Niall Ferguson, Brian Cathcart) find no market-rigging windfall and no Rothschild control of the 1815 press. No document links Mayer Amschel Rothschild to Frank or Weishaupt, and the idea of Rothschild control over nations and wars is one of the oldest antisemitic conspiracy tropes.

### Benjamin Disraeli

### The Illuminati

*Who:* The Order of the Illuminati was founded in Bavaria in 1776 by Adam Weishaupt, a Jesuit-educated law professor at Ingolstadt, as a secret society of Enlightenment reformers that recruited inside Masonic lodges. Bavaria banned it in 1784–85 and it collapsed.

**What he teaches**
- In SH END he offers a "theory": Mayer Rothschild introduced Jacob Frank to Weishaupt, "a former Jesuit" who was "also a Jew", and together they created the Illuminati, "what controls the world" [SH END · 02:51](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=171), [SH END · 03:48](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=228), [SH END · 04:27](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=267).
- The Illuminati then infiltrated the Freemasons, uniting Jesuits, Jews, Frankists, Catholics and Masons. The Masons allowed it because their end-time plan needs Jews back in Jerusalem: "They need Jews in place" [SH END · 04:57](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=297), [SH END · 40:13](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=2413).
- He relays the view that the ban was Weishaupt's own publicity stunt, and says the order "still exists today" [SH END · 05:31](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=331).
- By 2026 his account changes. In GT 26 the Templar-to-Freemason-to-Illuminati chain is a "metaphorical" story with "no evidence"; in New York the Illuminati is simply what you get when Freemasons, Jesuits and the Jewish diaspora cooperate, and "I'm not saying that they're planning this out" [GT 26 · 05:23](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=323), [GT 26 · 20:18](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=1218), [Meet-Up 3 · 48:01](https://youtu.be/5rDMq-5_xkM?t=2881), [Meet-Up 3 · 48:18](https://youtu.be/5rDMq-5_xkM?t=2898).

**In his big picture:** The Illuminati is the knot tying his separate networks (Frankist, Jesuit, Masonic, banking) together.

**In his words:** "we don't have any evidence that these three actually know each other, but this is a theory" [SH END · 03:48](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=228)

**Reality check:** Weishaupt was taught by Jesuits but was never one, and he was not Jewish. The idea of a surviving Illuminati comes from books of the late 1790s (Augustin Barruel, John Robison) that blamed it for the French Revolution, and no document links Weishaupt to Frank, who settled at Offenbach only in 1786, after the ban, or to Rothschild.

### The Knights Templar

*Who:* A Catholic military order founded in Jerusalem around 1119 to protect pilgrims. It ran an international financial network, was arrested in France from 1307 and dissolved by the pope in 1312, and its last Grand Master, Jacques de Molay, was burned in 1314.

**What he teaches**
- He calls them Europe's first multinational organization and in effect a bank: pilgrims deposited money at home and collected it in Jerusalem [Civ 40 · 37:40](https://youtu.be/3751ZjwmrBw?t=2260), [SH 24 · 1:13:49](https://youtu.be/TEdVhyE8pO0?t=4429).
- In March 2025 he argued that living among Jews and Muslims made them tolerant "freethinkers", that the Satan-worship charge had "no evidence", and that their suppression drove their ideas underground to incubate the Protestant Reformation and influence the American Revolution [Civ 40 · 40:11](https://youtu.be/3751ZjwmrBw?t=2411), [Civ 40 · 41:03](https://youtu.be/3751ZjwmrBw?t=2463).
- In May 2026 he calls them "probably the most successful secret society in the world" and lays out the popular conspiracy theory (Templars reborn as Scottish Rite Freemasons, creators of modernity, now steering toward World War III and summoning demons), then rejects it as without evidence and "anti-historical" [GT 26 · 00:43](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=43), [GT 26 · 02:06](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=126), [GT 26 · 05:23](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=323).
- He explains the legend as Masonic self-mythology: secret societies invent ancient pedigrees for legitimacy, as Pike did in claiming Templar descent [GT 26 · 17:13](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=1033), [GT 26 · 34:02](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=2042).

**In his big picture:** The Templars show him separating history from myth: a real transnational bank-and-army that later secret societies claimed as ancestors.

**In his words:** "This is a rewriting of history. It is none of this is true, okay?" [GT 26 · 34:02](https://youtu.be/RG1clZlrfOo?t=2042)

**Reality check:** The Templars' banking role and brutal suppression are well established, and historians regard the idolatry charges, extracted largely under torture, as baseless. Masonic "Templar" degrees date only from the 18th century, and there is no support for the Templars secretly surviving or for his 2025 claim that they incubated the Reformation.

### The Cathars

*Who:* A dualist Christian movement strong in southern France and northern Italy in the 12th and 13th centuries, crushed by the Albigensian Crusade (1209–29) and the Inquisition.

**What he teaches**
- He explains their dualism: the devil made this world of pain and trapped stolen souls in bodies, so life is an escape from prison. ### The Khazars

*Who:* A Turkic people whose khaganate dominated the steppe between the Black and Caspian Seas from about the 7th to the 10th century. Its ruling elite adopted Judaism, probably in the 8th or 9th century.

**What he teaches**
- Drawing on Arthur Koestler's *The Thirteenth Tribe* (1976), he says the Khazars chose Judaism to stay independent of both Byzantium and the Abbasids, and that persecuted Jews then fled to Khazaria as a homeland [SH END · 50:53](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3053), [SH END · 52:24](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3144), [SH END · 55:57](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3357).

- He divides Jews into Sephardim and Mizrahim, "real Jews", and Ashkenazim, 80% of the world's Jews: "they're not real Jews. They're converted". Their claim that God promised them the land is therefore, he says, "nonsense" [SH END · 1:01:43](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3703), [SH END · 1:02:14](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3734).
- He claims the subject is suppressed because it embarrasses Israel: "Unfortunately, you're not allowed to discuss this." [SH END · 58:00](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3480).

**In his big picture:** The Khazar thesis serves his Pax Judaica argument by cutting modern Israel's claim off from biblical descent and treating Jewish identity as a political choice made by an empire.

**In his words:** "The Jews, most of them don't come from Israel." [SH END · 50:53](https://youtu.be/WFWizN3QoPg?t=3053)

**Reality check:** Most genetic studies find that Ashkenazi ancestry combines Levantine and European (largely southern European) sources with no significant Khazar component. Koestler's thesis, revived by a minority of writers, is rejected by most historians and is widely used to deny Jewish ties to the land of Israel. Khazaria fell to the Kievan Rus' in the 960s, not the Mongols, converts are fully Jewish in Jewish law, and the question is openly debated rather than banned.
